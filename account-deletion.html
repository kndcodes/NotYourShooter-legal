Account & Data Deletion

For the mobile game Not Your Shooter (Android package com.knfused.notyourshootergame)
Published by Kanad Patil · Effective date: 26 October 2026

In short: You can ask us to delete your account and the game data saved to it at any time. Send one email and we will remove it, keeping only the limited records the law requires.

1. How to request deletion

Email knd.codes@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".

  • If you signed in with Google, please send the request from the same Google account (or tell us the email address) you use in the game, so we can locate your data.
  • If you only ever played as an anonymous (not signed-in) user, your progress lives on your device and in a temporary anonymous cloud record. Tell us and we will remove the anonymous cloud record; uninstalling the app clears the on-device copy.

We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

2. What gets deleted

On your request we delete your Firebase account and the associated cloud save held in Firebase Firestore, including:

  • Scores and high score
  • Levels completed and progress
  • In-game currency ("ingots")
  • Unlocked ships and entitlements
  • Your Google account identifier, name and email as stored for your account
  • Your push-notification (FCM) registration, where present

3. What we may keep

We retain a limited set of records only where we must, and for no longer than necessary:

DataWhy it is keptHow long
Purchase / transaction records Legal, tax and accounting obligations, and fraud prevention. Payment card details are never held by us — they are handled by Google Play. As required by law, then deleted
Crash diagnostics Already anonymized / not tied to your account; expire automatically. Firebase default retention

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — your progress cannot be restored afterwards.
