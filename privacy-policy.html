Privacy Policy

For the mobile game Not Your Shooter (Android package com.knfused.notyourshootergame)
Published by Kanad Patil · Effective date: 26 October 2026

In short: Not Your Shooter is a single-player arcade game. We collect the minimum data needed to save your progress across devices, process optional purchases, keep the game stable, and (for free players) show ads. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

This app is developed and operated by Kanad Patil ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, how it is shared, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the game's features require. The categories below reflect the services the app actually uses.

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Account & sign-in An anonymous Firebase user ID is created automatically. If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile basics from Google. To create your account and let you restore progress on other devices.
Game progress (cloud save) Scores, levels completed, in-game currency ("ingots"), unlocked ships, and entitlements. To back up and sync your progress via Firebase Firestore.
Purchases & entitlements Records of in-app purchases (e.g. ingot packs, bundles, ad-removal), purchase tokens and grant records. Payment card details are handled by Google Play — we never see them. To deliver and verify what you bought and restore it later.
Diagnostics & crash data Crash logs, error traces and basic device/app state via Firebase Crashlytics. To find and fix bugs and keep the game stable.
Push notifications A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token and a subscription to a general players topic. To send game updates and announcements (with your OS-level permission).
Advertising For players who have not purchased ad-removal, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may use an advertising identifier and related device signals. To display ads that keep the game free.
Motion / sensor data Accelerometer readings via the device's motion sensors. Used in real time on your device for tilt-based gameplay. It is not transmitted or stored by us.

We do not intentionally collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide core gameplay and save/restore your progress across devices.
  • Process, verify and restore in-app purchases and entitlements.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • Send optional service and update notifications.
  • Show ads to players who have not removed them.
  • Enforce our Terms, prevent fraud/abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that operate parts of the game on our behalf:

  • Google / Firebase — Authentication, Firestore (cloud saves), Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Remote Config.
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) — serving ads to non-paying players.
  • Google Play Billing — processing in-app purchases.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process personal data on these bases: performance of a contract (providing the game and purchases), legitimate interests (stability, security, fraud prevention, and non-personalized ads), consent (notifications and personalized ads, where applicable), and legal obligation.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and cloud-save data for as long as your account exists so you can restore progress. Purchase and grant records are retained as needed for delivery, support and legal/accounting requirements. Crash diagnostics are retained for a limited period per Firebase defaults. When you delete your account, associated cloud data is deleted or anonymized, except records we must keep by law.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correction, deletion: You may request a copy of your data, corrections, or deletion of your account and cloud save by contacting us (see below). Step-by-step instructions are on our Account & Data Deletion page.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Ads: You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device's ad settings, or purchase ad-removal in-app.
  • Google sign-in: You can continue to play as an anonymous user instead of signing in with Google.
  • EEA/UK & California residents have additional rights (including the right to object, restrict processing, data portability, and, for California, to opt out of "sale"/"sharing" — which we do not do). Contact us to exercise them.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are readable only by the signed-in owner, and progress writes and paid entitlements are processed server-side through trusted Cloud Functions so a device cannot grant itself paid items. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children's privacy

This game is not directed at children under the age required for data processing in your region (for example, under 13 in the United States or under 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where the game is served to children, ads and data use are limited accordingly.

10. International data transfers

Our providers (such as Google/Firebase) may process and store data on servers located in other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
